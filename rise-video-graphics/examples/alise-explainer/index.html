Claude Duet is a personal Claude you simply text, like a teammate who already knows your week.
It reads your connected Gmail, calendar and Drive.
It keeps your tasks in one list and sends you a morning brief.
It checks in when something needs you, and handles the longer work in the background while you keep moving.
Text it: who still owes receipts this month?
It pulls the answer from your mail and sheets, and drafts a friendly nudge for each department.
Before each department budget meeting, it hands you a one-page brief built from the emails and spreadsheets you already have.
For events like the serve team Christmas party,
it tracks the to-dos, holds the dates, and drafts the follow-up email for you to send.
And before Tuesday’s staff meeting, it turns your updates into ready proposals and FYIs,
so the meeting stays about decisions.
Every step that sends, pays, or can’t be undone waits for your yes.
And money always goes to a person for approval. Claude never buys anything on its own.
